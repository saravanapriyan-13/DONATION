Simple Process

How ShareSphere Works

Four simple steps to share or receive resources with zero cost.

📸
1. List Unused Items

Snap a photo of books, food, clothes, or electronics and publish in 60 seconds.

📍
2. Match Within 10 km

Neighbors discover nearby items using GPS distance matching & interactive maps.

💬
3. Connect & Schedule

Request the resource, chat in real-time, and agree on a safe pickup time.

🌱
4. Handover & Earn Badges

Complete handover, earn Impact Points, and celebrate community sustainability!
