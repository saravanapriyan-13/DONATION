Methodology & Assumptions Notice

*Environmental values are scientific estimations provided for transparency and community encouragement.

Conversion factors: Average electronics re-use saves ~8.5 kg CO₂e and ~2.5 kg landfill footprint; furniture reuse saves ~15.0 kg CO₂e; books and paper products save ~1.8 kg CO₂e; food redistribution prevents ~2.2 kg CO₂e per surplus meal by averting methane generation in decomposing landfills.
